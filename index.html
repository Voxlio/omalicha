MY OMALICHA

HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO MY EVERYTHING

Happy birthday to the woman who holds such a special place in my heart. Before anything else, I want you to know how grateful I am that you exist, and that out of everyone in this world, I get to call you mine.

I know things haven't been perfect between us. We've disagreed, misunderstood each other, said things we didn't mean. I won't pretend those moments didn't hurt. But through all of it, one thing hasn't changed: I still care about you deeply. I still see the woman I fell in love with — the one whose voice changes my mood, whose happiness genuinely matters to me, who I want to see win in life.

Today isn't about our problems. It's about you — your strength, your beauty, your stubbornness, your dreams, everything that makes you who you are.

I pray this new chapter brings you real happiness, peace that doesn't depend on circumstance, and doors opening where you least expect them. I pray you meet people who genuinely want to see you succeed, and that you have the wisdom, strength, and courage for whatever comes next.

I don't know exactly what the future holds for us, but I know what I want: for us to communicate better, forgive faster, listen more, and love each other without either of us feeling alone. Not perfect — just real. Two people who can hurt each other by accident and still find their way back.

"I believe what we've built deserves patience, and that love isn't only about the easy days — sometimes it's having the courage to say, 'we're not okay right now, but I don't want to give up on us.' That's how I feel about you."

I also want to say sorry for the times I've made you feel hurt, ignored, or unloved. I don't always get it right. But I'm learning, and I want this new chapter to teach us both how to love each other better — not just in words, in actions.

I hope today reminds you how valuable you are. I hope you see what I see: a woman with real potential, still becoming who she's meant to be, who deserves good things and deep love.

Thank you for the memories, the laughter, the conversations, and for being part of my life. Thank you for being my Omalicha.

If I could make one wish for you, it's this: that this chapter brings you closer to the woman you've always wanted to become — and that I get to be beside you as you become her.

So today, forget the arguments, just for today. Smile. Eat your cake. Take pictures. Feel loved, because you deserve to.

Happy birthday, my Omalicha. I love you, and I'm willing to keep trying, keep growing, and keep fighting for us — because I believe we can always choose to rise together.

Here's to another beautiful year of your life, and to more beautiful chapters of us. ✨
